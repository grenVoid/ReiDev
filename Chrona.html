Chrona

SUMMARY

Chrona is a productivity and scheduling tool created for students, personal users, and employees who manage heavy workloads and many schedules. It allows users to set tasks, create reminders, and use device notifications and alarms as additional support for keeping track of responsibilities.

INFORMATION

Inside Chrona

A closer look at the purpose, status system, reminders, and design philosophy behind the project.

01

What is Chrona?

Chrona was created for people who have to manage multiple schedules, tasks, and responsibilities throughout the day. It can be used by students managing academic work, employees handling workloads, or individuals organizing personal tasks.

The application allows users to set schedules and reminders while using device notifications and alarms as additional support. The goal is to make important tasks easier to remember without removing the responsibility from the person doing them.

02

Task Status

Chrona records how a scheduled task was completed and provides a different status depending on what happened. A task can be marked as completed, completed late, or missed.

Each status is paired with its own message designed to remind the user about responsibility and time management instead of simply showing a technical result.

COMPLETED Task finished as scheduled.
COMPLETED LATE Task was finished after the scheduled time.
MISSED Task was not completed.
03

Reminders

Chrona can use device permissions to provide notifications and alarm support. These features are intended to help users remember scheduled responsibilities even when they are focused on other activities.

The technology acts as a reminder and support system, while the actual task remains the responsibility of the user.

SCHEDULES REMINDERS NOTIFICATIONS ALARMS TASK SUPPORT
04

Design Philosophy

Chrona was created around the idea that technology should support people rather than replace their responsibility. The application does not attempt to perform the physical work for the user.

Instead, it helps users manage their time, remember important tasks, and keep track of whether those responsibilities were completed. The technology becomes a support tool while the person remains responsible for the work itself.

CORE IDEA TECHNOLOGY SUPPORTS. PEOPLE DO THE WORK.
