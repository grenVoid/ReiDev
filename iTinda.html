SOFTWARE PROJECT

iTinda

SUMMARY

iTinda is a point-of-sale system designed for local small businesses that still rely on manual inventory and sales tracking. It helps manage products, monitor stocks, and record sales while giving store owners a clearer view of what their community actually needs. By understanding which products sell and which do not, iTinda helps reduce unnecessary purchases and prevent stock from going to waste.

Inside iTinda

01

What is iTinda?

iTinda is a point-of-sale system created to help local business owners manage their products, customers, and sales transactions more efficiently. Beyond recording purchases, the system can provide useful data about what customers buy, how frequently products are sold, and how sales move through the store.

Many small local stores still rely on manual inventory and limited ways of understanding what products are actually needed in their community. At the same time, some consumers increasingly turn to online shops and businesses outside their local area, making it more difficult for small store owners to identify changing demand without surveys or sufficient purchase data.

iTinda was created around this problem. By organizing sales, product, and inventory records, it can help local store owners better understand purchasing patterns and make more informed decisions about what to stock. The goal is to help stores avoid unnecessary purchases, reduce unsold products, and better respond to the needs of the communities they serve.

02

Features

Every completed transaction can be backed up automatically, giving the store a reliable record that can still be checked when printed documents are unavailable or when resources such as printers are limited.

Inventory monitoring can also provide color-based warnings when product stocks become insufficient. At the end of a transaction, the owner can choose whether the system should use a printer or keep the records digitally, depending on the resources available in the store.

Security features help protect important account actions. Gmail-based confirmation can be used when changing a password, registering a trusted account, or adding an employee or relative. The system can also keep track of account logins and verify that registered accounts exist before allowing access.

The application can be customized with the store's own name, while built-in settings provide guidance for configuration, key shortcuts, and other available system options.

Development

The application was developed using C# with SQLite for local data storage. The project helped me work with application logic, database operations, interface design, and real-world software workflows.
